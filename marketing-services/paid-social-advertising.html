What is paid social advertising?

Paid social advertising means running paid adverts on social media platforms. The main platforms for service businesses are Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn.

Unlike search advertising, paid social does not wait for someone to search. It places your business in front of people who match your ideal customer profile.

This makes paid social ideal for building awareness, promoting new services and reaching customers before they start comparing suppliers.

It is also highly measurable. You can track every click, lead and enquiry back to the advert that produced it.

Here at ICE Media Group, we plan, create and manage paid social campaigns for service businesses across the UK.

Paid social works best as part of a wider plan. We often combine it with Google Ads, SEO and content to reach customers at every stage.

We work with businesses in Kent, London and across the UK, from local trades to national service providers.

Every campaign starts with a clear plan, agreed budgets and measurable targets.

LinkedIn advertising for business-to-business services

LinkedIn is the leading platform for reaching business decision makers. It allows you to target by job title, seniority, industry, company size and location.

This is valuable for business-to-business services such as commercial cleaning, facilities management, maintenance contracts and professional services.

For example, a campaign can reach facilities managers and operations directors at companies with more than 50 employees in London and Kent.

We use LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms to make enquiring easy. These forms are pre-filled with the user's profile details, which increases response rates.

We also use LinkedIn to share case studies and thought leadership. This builds trust with buyers during longer decision cycles.

Facebook and Instagram advertising for consumer services

Facebook and Instagram, both owned by Meta, reach a large share of UK adults. They are effective for consumer and home services.

We target homeowners and residents by location, age, interests and behaviour. We can also target people who have visited your website or engaged with your content.

Clear offers perform best on these platforms. Examples include free surveys, fixed-price services or seasonal promotions.

We use Meta lead forms and website conversion campaigns, depending on your goals. Both can be connected directly to your inbox or CRM.

We install and configure the Meta Pixel and Conversions API. These tools give accurate tracking and help Meta's system find more people likely to enquire.

Creative that stops the scroll

On social media, your advert competes with friends, family and entertainment. Strong creative is the biggest factor in a campaign's success.

We produce static images, carousels and short-form videos for each campaign. We write headlines and copy designed to grab attention quickly.

Real photos and videos from your jobs usually outperform stock images. We will guide your team on what to capture on site.

We test several creative versions at the same time. We measure cost per lead for each and move budget towards the best performers.

Creative fatigue happens when audiences see the same advert too often. We refresh adverts regularly to keep results strong.

All creative follows your brand guidelines, including colours, fonts, tone of voice and approved claims.

Retargeting and full-funnel campaigns

Most website visitors do not enquire on their first visit. Retargeting shows adverts to people who have already visited your website or engaged with your content.

These audiences already know your business, so they are more likely to enquire. Retargeting often produces some of the lowest-cost leads in a campaign.

We build full-funnel campaigns that guide people from first awareness to enquiry. Each stage uses different messages and formats.

For example, a short video introduces your service, a case study builds trust and a clear offer prompts the enquiry.

This joined-up approach works especially well alongside Google Ads and SEO.

Lead quality and follow-up

A low cost per lead only matters if the leads become customers. We focus on lead quality from the start of every campaign.

We add qualifying questions to lead forms. These can confirm the service needed, the location, the timescale or the size of the job.

Leads are delivered instantly to your inbox, CRM or a shared spreadsheet. Fast follow-up is essential, as response rates fall quickly after the first hour.

We can also set up automatic confirmation emails or messages. These reassure the customer that their enquiry has been received.

We review lead quality with your team regularly. Your feedback helps us refine targeting, adverts and form questions.

Where possible, we track which social leads become paying jobs. This shows the true value of your paid social investment.

This joined-up approach makes sure campaigns are judged on revenue, not just lead numbers.

Video advertising on social media

Short-form video is one of the most effective formats on social media. It captures attention quickly and explains services clearly.

Videos do not need large production budgets. Clips filmed on a phone during real jobs often perform better than polished studio content.

We edit footage into short adverts for Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn. Each video is sized correctly for its placement, including stories and reels.

We add captions to every video. Many people watch social media with the sound off.

Before-and-after footage works especially well for service businesses. It shows the result in seconds.

We also create short explainer videos for more complex services.

Video performance is tracked alongside other creative, so budget always goes to the best performers.

FAQs

Paid social advertising: common questions

Which platform is right for my business?

LinkedIn suits business-to-business services. Facebook and Instagram suit consumer services. We will advise based on your customers.

Do you create the ads?

Yes. We produce the images, video and copy. We can also use your own photos and footage.

Are social leads lower quality than search leads?

They can be, if campaigns are not set up carefully. We use qualifying questions on lead forms to improve quality.

How quickly will I see results?

Campaigns usually produce leads within the first two weeks. The testing phase then lowers your cost per lead over time.

What budget do I need for paid social advertising?

Budgets depend on your audience size and goals. We recommend a test budget that allows enough data to compare creative and audiences, then scale what works.

Can you manage our organic social media as well?

Yes. Our Content and Social service covers organic posting. Many clients combine both for a consistent presence.

Do I need a Facebook or LinkedIn page to advertise?

Yes. Each platform requires a business page to run adverts. If you do not have one, we can set it up for you as part of onboarding.

Will you need access to our social media accounts?

We need partner access to your ad accounts and business pages. You keep full ownership, and access can be removed at any time.
