What is Google Ads management?

Google Ads is Google's advertising platform. It lets your business appear at the top of search results when customers search for your services.

You pay when someone clicks your advert. This model is often called pay-per-click, or PPC.

Google Ads management is the ongoing work of planning, building and improving your campaigns. It covers keywords, ads, bids, budgets, landing pages and tracking.

A well-managed account produces a steady flow of enquiries at a predictable cost. A poorly managed account can spend thousands of pounds with little to show for it.

Here at ICE Media Group, we manage Google Ads for service businesses. Our aim is to lower your cost per lead while increasing the number of quality enquiries.

We manage campaigns for businesses across Kent, London and the South East, as well as national service providers.

Whether you need emergency call-outs, planned maintenance contracts or high-value projects, we build campaigns around the work you want more of.

Every account starts with a free audit, so you can see our recommendations before you commit.

Search campaigns built around high-intent keywords

Search campaigns show text adverts to people actively searching on Google. They are the most effective Google Ads format for most service businesses.

We research the keywords your customers use when they are ready to book. Examples include 'blocked drain repair near me' or 'office cleaning contract London'.

We group keywords into tightly themed campaigns and ad groups. Each group has adverts written specifically for those searches.

We also build a detailed list of negative keywords. These stop your adverts appearing for irrelevant searches, such as jobs, training or DIY guides.

Negative keywords are one of the quickest ways to reduce wasted spend. We review your search terms every week and add new negatives as needed.

We use ad extensions, now called assets, to make your adverts larger and more useful. These include call buttons, site links, locations and key selling points.

Local Services Ads and Performance Max

Local Services Ads appear above standard Google Ads for many trade and home services. They show a Google Screened or Google Guaranteed badge.

With Local Services Ads, you pay per lead rather than per click. This can make them very cost-effective for eligible businesses.

We handle the setup and verification process for you. We then manage your service areas, budgets, reviews and lead disputes.

Performance Max campaigns use Google's automation to show ads across Search, Maps, YouTube and other placements. They can work well, but they need careful setup and monitoring.

We only recommend Performance Max where it suits your goals. We always set it up with accurate conversion data and clear controls.

Conversion tracking: knowing what every lead costs

Accurate conversion tracking is the foundation of successful Google Ads management. Without it, you cannot tell which keywords and adverts produce enquiries.

We track phone calls from your adverts and your website. We also track contact forms, quote requests, emails and live chat enquiries.

Where possible, we import offline conversions. This tells Google which leads became paying jobs, so it can find more customers like them.

Good tracking also improves Google's automated bidding. Smart bidding strategies only work well when they receive reliable conversion data.

Every client can see their cost per lead by campaign, keyword and location. This makes budget decisions simple and evidence-based.

Landing pages that turn clicks into enquiries

Sending paid traffic to your homepage is a common and costly mistake. Visitors need a page that matches the exact service they searched for.

We design focused landing pages for your key services. Each page has a clear headline, trust signals, service details and a simple contact form.

Fast loading speed is essential, especially on mobile. Slow pages lose visitors before they see your offer.

We test different headlines, layouts and calls to action. Small improvements in conversion rate can significantly reduce your cost per lead.

Better landing pages also improve your Quality Score in Google Ads. A higher Quality Score can lower the price you pay for each click.

Budget management and smart bidding

Your budget should go where it produces the most profitable enquiries. We allocate spend by service, location and time of day based on real performance data.

We use Google's smart bidding strategies where they suit your account. These include Maximise Conversions and Target Cost Per Acquisition (CPA).

Smart bidding needs accurate conversion data to work well. We make sure tracking is reliable before switching to automated bidding.

We also use ad scheduling. If your team cannot answer calls at certain times, we can reduce or pause adverts during those hours.

Location targeting is refined to match the areas you cover. We can increase bids in your most profitable postcodes and exclude areas you do not serve.

Seasonal businesses need flexible budgets. We plan increases ahead of busy periods and reduce spend when demand drops.

You approve every budget change. We never increase your spend without your agreement.

FAQs

Google Ads management: common questions

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your services and area. We will recommend a starting budget after reviewing search volumes and competitor activity.

Is the ad spend included in your fee?

No. You pay Google directly for ad spend. Our management fee is separate, so you always see what goes to Google.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. We work inside your account, and you keep full access and all data if you stop working with us.

Can you take over my existing account?

Yes. We start with a full audit and keep anything that is already working well.

What is a good cost per lead for Google Ads?

It varies by service, location and competition. We benchmark your current results and set realistic targets during the audit, then work to lower your cost per lead over time.

How quickly can my campaigns go live?

Most new campaigns go live within two to three weeks. This allows time for tracking setup, keyword research and building the adverts properly.

Do you work with a minimum ad spend?

We do not set a strict minimum. However, we will advise if your budget is too low to compete effectively in your market, so you can make an informed decision.

Can you run Google Ads alongside our SEO?

Yes. Google Ads and SEO work well together. Ads produce leads quickly while SEO builds long-term visibility, and data from each channel improves the other.
