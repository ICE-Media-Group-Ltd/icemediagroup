What is SEO and why does it matter for service businesses?

Search engine optimisation (SEO) is the process of improving your website so it appears higher in Google's organic results. Organic results are the listings below the paid ads and the map.

For service businesses, SEO is one of the most valuable marketing channels. Customers who search for a service are usually ready to make an enquiry.

A customer searching 'commercial cleaning company Dartford' or 'emergency plumber Bromley' has a clear need. If your business appears first, you are likely to get the call.

Unlike paid advertising, you do not pay for each click from organic search. The work you invest in SEO keeps producing enquiries month after month.

Here at ICE Media Group, we provide SEO services for trades, facilities, property and professional service companies. Our focus is simple: more qualified enquiries from Google.

Our SEO services cover technical SEO, local SEO, content, link building and reporting.

We can work on a single service or manage your full organic search strategy.

Technical SEO: building the right foundations

Technical SEO makes sure Google can find, crawl and understand every page on your website. Without it, even excellent content can fail to rank.

We start every project with a full technical SEO audit. We check page speed, mobile usability, indexing, site structure, broken links and duplicate content.

We also review your Core Web Vitals. These are Google's measures of loading speed, stability and responsiveness, and they affect rankings.

Next, we fix the issues we find. This can include compressing images, improving internal links, adding structured data and correcting redirects.

Structured data, also called schema markup, helps Google understand your business. It can show extra details in search results, such as reviews, opening hours and FAQs.

Once the foundations are in place, every new page you publish has a better chance of ranking quickly.

Local SEO: winning searches in the areas you cover

Most service businesses work within a defined area. Local SEO helps you appear when customers in that area search for your services.

Your Google Business Profile is central to local SEO. We optimise your categories, services, photos, descriptions and posts so your profile appears in the map results.

We also build and clean up your business citations. These are listings of your business name, address and phone number on directories across the web.

Consistent citations help Google trust your location details. Inconsistent or outdated listings can hold your rankings back.

For businesses covering several towns, we create dedicated location pages. Each page explains the services you offer in that area, with useful local detail.

We also help you generate more Google reviews from real customers. Reviews influence both your map rankings and the number of people who choose to call you.

Keyword research and SEO content that converts

Effective SEO starts with understanding what your customers type into Google. We research the keywords that match your services, locations and customer needs.

We focus on keywords with commercial intent. These are searches made by people looking to buy or book a service, not just to read about a topic.

We then map each keyword to the right page on your website. Every core service should have its own page, written to answer customer questions clearly.

Our SEO content is written for people first and search engines second. It is clear, factual and easy to read on a phone.

We also publish supporting articles that answer common questions. These help build your authority and bring in visitors at earlier stages of their decision.

Every page includes a clear call to action, so visitors know how to contact you.

Link building and authority

Google uses links from other websites as a signal of trust. A link from a relevant, respected website tells Google your business is credible.

We build links through genuine, safe methods. These include industry directories, local business organisations, supplier partnerships and digital PR.

We never buy links or use low-quality link schemes. These tactics can lead to penalties and long-term damage to your rankings.

We also review your existing backlink profile. If we find harmful links, we recommend the right action to protect your website.

Over time, a stronger link profile helps all your pages rank higher, including new service and location pages.

SEO for multi-location and multi-service businesses

Businesses that cover several towns or offer many services need a clear SEO structure. Without one, pages compete with each other and none of them rank well.

We build a logical website hierarchy. Core service pages sit at the top, with supporting location and sub-service pages beneath them.

Each location page is written with genuine local detail. We include the areas served, relevant projects, local accreditations and useful information for customers in that area.

We avoid thin, copied location pages. Google treats near-duplicate pages as low quality, and they rarely rank.

Internal links connect related services and locations. This helps visitors find what they need and helps Google understand which pages matter most.

For businesses with several branches, we set up and optimise a Google Business Profile for each genuine location.

This structured approach allows you to grow into new areas and services without starting your SEO from scratch each time.

FAQs

SEO services: common questions

How long does SEO take to work?

Most clients see early movement within three months. Strong results usually take six to twelve months, depending on competition.

Do you guarantee first-place rankings?

No. Nobody can guarantee rankings on Google. We commit to clear work each month and transparent reporting on results.

Will you need access to my website?

Yes. We need admin access to make technical changes and publish content. We can also work with your existing web developer.

Is there a minimum contract?

We recommend six months to see meaningful results. After that, you can continue on a rolling monthly basis.

What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads?

Google Ads places paid adverts at the top of search results, and you pay for each click. SEO improves your organic listings, which you do not pay for per click. Most service businesses benefit from using both together.

Do you work with businesses outside Kent?

Yes. We are based in Dartford and work with many businesses in Kent, London and the South East. We also provide SEO services to companies across the UK.

What does an SEO audit include?

Our SEO audit covers technical health, page speed, mobile usability, keyword rankings, content gaps, local SEO and backlinks. You receive a clear, prioritised list of recommendations.

Can I do some SEO work in-house?

Yes. We can work alongside your team, handling technical and strategic work while your staff contribute content, photos and reviews. We will provide clear guidance.
