Why your website matters more than ever

Your website is usually the first place a customer checks before contacting you. It shapes their first impression of your business within seconds.

Customers find your website through Google searches, adverts, social media and referrals. Wherever they come from, the website needs to convince them to get in touch.

An outdated, slow or confusing website loses enquiries every day. Many visitors leave and contact a competitor instead.

A well-designed website does the opposite. It explains your services clearly, builds trust and makes enquiring simple.

Here at ICE Media Group, we design and build websites for service businesses. Every website we create is focused on one goal: generating enquiries.

We design websites for trades, cleaning, facilities, drainage, property and professional service companies.

Every website we build is designed to support SEO, Google Ads and social media from launch.

Whether you need a simple five-page website or a large site with many service and location pages, we follow the same proven process.

Conversion-focused website design

Conversion-focused design means every page is built to guide visitors towards an enquiry. Good looks matter, but results matter more.

We start by understanding your customers and how they choose a supplier. We then plan the website structure around your core services.

Each service has its own page with clear information, benefits and next steps. This helps customers and search engines understand exactly what you offer.

We place calls to action in the right places. Phone numbers, quote forms and booking buttons are always easy to find.

Trust signals are essential for service businesses. We feature accreditations, reviews, case studies and photos of your real work.

We keep forms short and simple. Every extra field reduces the number of people who complete them.

Mobile-first, fast and accessible

Most local service searches now happen on mobile phones. We design every website for mobile first, then adapt it for larger screens.

Speed is critical. Slow websites frustrate visitors, reduce enquiries and can lower your Google rankings.

We optimise images, code and hosting to keep loading times fast. We test every website against Google's Core Web Vitals before launch.

We also follow accessibility best practice. This includes readable text sizes, strong colour contrast and clear navigation for all users.

An accessible website reaches more customers and reflects well on your business.

SEO built in from day one

A new website is the best opportunity to get your SEO foundations right. We build SEO into every stage of the project.

We plan page structure and URLs around the keywords your customers use. Each page has a unique title, meta description and heading structure.

We add structured data to help Google understand your business, services and location. We also create an XML sitemap and connect Google Search Console.

If you are replacing an existing website, we protect your current rankings. We map every old page to its new equivalent and set up permanent redirects.

This careful approach avoids the traffic drops that often happen when websites are rebuilt without SEO planning.

Easy to manage, fully supported

Your website should be easy to update. We build on content management systems that let your team edit text, images and pages without technical skills.

At launch, we provide training and a simple guide. Your team will know how to make everyday changes with confidence.

We also offer monthly hosting and support plans. These cover security updates, backups, uptime monitoring and small content changes.

Tracking is set up before launch. We configure Google Analytics, Search Console, call tracking and form tracking.

From the first day, you can see how many enquiries your new website produces and where they come from.

Our website design process

Every website project follows a clear, structured process. This keeps the project on time and makes sure the finished website meets your goals.

We start with a discovery session. We learn about your business, customers, competitors and the results you want the website to deliver.

Next, we agree a sitemap and content plan. This sets out every page, its purpose and the keywords it will target.

We then design key page layouts for your review. You can provide feedback before any development begins.

Once the design is approved, we build the website and add your content. We can write the content for you or work with text you provide.

Before launch, we test every page on different devices and browsers. We check forms, tracking, speed and redirects.

After launch, we monitor performance closely and make any final adjustments. Ongoing support is available to keep the website secure and up to date.

Website features for service businesses

Service businesses need specific features to turn visitors into enquiries. We include these as standard where they suit your business.

Click-to-call buttons make it easy for mobile visitors to phone you. Quote and booking forms capture the details your team needs.

Service area pages show customers which locations you cover. They also support local SEO.

Review widgets display your latest Google reviews. Accreditation badges show your industry memberships and certifications.

Project galleries and case studies give visitors proof of your work.

We can also add live chat, online booking, downloadable brochures and recruitment pages.

Every feature is tested for speed and usability before launch.

FAQs

Website design: common questions

How long does a new website take?

Most websites take six to eight weeks. Larger websites with many service or location pages can take longer.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

Yes. We provide training and a short guide so your team can make everyday changes.

Will I lose my Google rankings when you rebuild?

Not if the move is managed properly. We map old pages to new ones and set up redirects to protect your rankings.

Do you offer hosting and support?

Yes. We offer monthly hosting, security updates and support. You can also host the website yourself.

How much does a new website cost?

Costs depend on the number of pages, features and content required. We provide a fixed quote after a free consultation, so there are no surprises.

Can you redesign my existing website instead of starting again?

Yes. If your current platform is sound, a redesign can be faster and more cost-effective. We will advise on the best option during the consultation.

Do you write the website content?

Yes. Our team can write all website content, optimised for SEO and written in your brand voice. We can also edit content your team provides.

Which platform do you build websites on?

We recommend a platform based on your needs, budget and how you want to manage content. We will explain the options clearly during the consultation.
