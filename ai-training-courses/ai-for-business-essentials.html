Course overview

A clear starting point for any team.

AI tools are now part of everyday work. Many teams are unsure where to start, what is safe to use and where the real benefits are.

This course gives your team a clear, practical understanding of AI for business. It avoids technical jargon and focuses on real tasks.

We explain how tools such as ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini and Claude work. We also cover their strengths, limitations and risks.

Every example comes from real business tasks. These include writing emails, summarising documents, preparing reports and answering customer questions.

By the end of the session, every attendee will have used AI tools with confidence. They will also leave with a list of tasks they can start using AI for straight away.

Here at ICE Media Group, we use AI across our own marketing and operations every day. This course shares what we have learned in practice.

In detail

Why AI training matters for your business

Many employees already use AI tools without guidance. This can lead to wasted time, inaccurate work and data protection risks.

Structured AI training gives everyone the same foundation. Your team learns to use AI effectively, safely and consistently.

Businesses that train their teams early tend to see faster benefits. Staff become more confident, and good habits are set from the start.

Training also helps leaders make better decisions. Once the team understands what AI can do, it is easier to plan where to invest next.

How the course is delivered

The course is delivered live by an experienced trainer. It is not a pre-recorded video course.

Sessions run online, or on site at your premises for private groups. Every attendee works through practical exercises during the session.

We keep groups small so everyone can ask questions and get support. Exercises can be tailored to your industry for private sessions.

After the course, attendees receive a summary guide and a set of example prompts to use in their own work.

Common uses of AI in a business setting

AI tools can support almost every department. The course shows practical examples from across a typical business.

Customer service teams use AI to draft replies, summarise complaints and suggest helpful responses. Sales teams use it to prepare proposals, research prospects and write follow-up emails.

Operations teams use AI to summarise job notes, prepare reports and create checklists. Finance and admin teams use it to explain spreadsheets, draft letters and tidy data.

Managers use AI to prepare meeting agendas, summarise long documents and plan projects.

Attendees choose the examples most relevant to their own role and practise them during the session.

Each example includes a clear explanation of where human review is still essential.

Understanding the limits of AI

AI tools are powerful, but they are not always right. They can produce information that sounds convincing but is incorrect.

This is often called a hallucination. It happens because AI predicts likely words rather than checking facts.

The course teaches simple ways to reduce these errors. These include giving clear context, asking for sources and checking key facts.

We also explain why AI should not make final decisions on legal, financial, safety or employment matters.

Understanding these limits helps your team use AI with confidence and avoid costly mistakes.

Attendees leave knowing when to trust AI output and when to check it carefully.

Getting started after the course

The first few weeks after training are the most important. Habits formed early tend to last.

We recommend each attendee chooses three tasks to try with AI in their first week. Simple, low-risk tasks such as drafting internal emails or summarising notes are ideal.

Teams should share what works in a short weekly catch-up. This spreads good practice quickly.

Managers should agree which AI tools are approved and how sensitive information is handled.

After a month, review which tasks have saved the most time and where further training would help.

We provide a simple 30-day plan to guide this process.

FAQs

Common questions

Do attendees need any technical knowledge?

No. The course is designed for complete beginners. If you can use email and a web browser, you can take part.

Which AI tools will we use?

We demonstrate the main tools, including ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini and Claude. Attendees can practise with free versions.

Can the course be tailored to our business?

Yes. Private sessions can use examples and exercises based on your industry, processes and documents.

Is the course suitable for large teams?

Yes. We can run several sessions for larger teams, or combine an all-staff briefing with smaller hands-on groups.

What should we do after the course?

Many teams move on to Prompting for everyday work or AI policy and safe use. We will recommend next steps based on your goals.

How many people can attend a session?

We recommend up to 12 people for hands-on sessions, so everyone gets support. Larger groups can be split across several sessions.

Do we need paid AI subscriptions?

No. Attendees can practise with free versions of the main AI tools. We explain the benefits of paid business plans during the session.
