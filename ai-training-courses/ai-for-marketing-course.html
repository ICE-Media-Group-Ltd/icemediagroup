In detail

AI and SEO: what marketing teams need to know

Search engines reward content that is helpful, accurate and original. Content produced purely by AI, without expert input, often fails to meet that standard.

This course shows how to use AI responsibly for SEO. AI supports research, structure and drafting, while your team adds expertise, detail and accuracy.

We cover how to write strong briefs, check facts and add genuine experience to every piece.

This approach helps your content rank and builds trust with readers.

Building AI into your marketing workflow

The biggest gains come from building AI into everyday workflows, not using it occasionally.

We help your team map their current processes and identify where AI fits. Common examples include first drafts, repurposing content and preparing reports.

We also cover quality control. Every workflow includes clear review steps before anything is published.

By the end of the course, your team will have a documented AI workflow that suits your business.

Creating a brand voice guide for AI

AI tools write in a neutral, generic style by default. A brand voice guide teaches them to write like your business.

During the course, each team creates a brand voice guide designed for AI. It covers tone, sentence style, preferred words, phrases to avoid and example copy.

We show how to load this guide into AI tools as custom instructions or project knowledge. Every draft then starts closer to your brand.

We also cover how to adapt the voice for different channels. Website copy, social posts and emails often need slightly different styles.

A good voice guide reduces editing time and keeps content consistent across the team.

Teams leave with a finished guide they can use immediately.

AI for paid advertising

Paid advertising needs many versions of headlines, descriptions and creative. AI can produce variations quickly for testing.

The course covers how to write Google Ads headlines and descriptions within character limits. We also cover social media ad copy and captions.

Attendees learn to generate variations based on different benefits, audiences and offers.

We explain how to review AI ad copy for accuracy and compliance. Claims must be truthful and supported, in line with UK advertising standards.

We also show how AI can help analyse ad performance and suggest new tests.

These techniques help teams test more ideas without increasing workload.

Measuring the impact of AI on your marketing

It is important to know whether AI is genuinely helping. The course shows how to measure its impact.

Simple measures include time saved per task, volume of content produced and the amount of editing needed.

Performance measures matter too. These include rankings, engagement, click-through rates and enquiries from AI-assisted content.

We help teams set a starting benchmark before introducing new workflows. This makes it easy to compare results later.

Regular reviews help teams refine their approach and focus AI on the tasks with the biggest gains.

Attendees leave with a simple tracking template for their team.

Repurposing content with AI

Creating new content from scratch takes time. Repurposing turns one strong piece into many.

The course shows how to use AI to adapt a single article or case study into social posts, email newsletters, ad copy and short video scripts.

Each format is adjusted for its channel. LinkedIn posts, Instagram captions and emails all need a different length, tone and structure.

We also cover how to update older content. AI can help identify outdated sections and suggest improvements.

Repurposing helps teams maintain a consistent presence without a matching increase in workload.

Attendees build a simple repurposing workflow during day two.

FAQs

Common questions

Will AI replace our marketing team?

No. AI handles repetitive tasks and first drafts. Your team remains responsible for strategy, creativity, accuracy and brand voice.

Does Google penalise AI-generated content?

Google focuses on content quality rather than how it is produced. Low-quality content can perform poorly, which is why this course focuses on human review and expertise.

Can the course include our own brand guidelines?

Yes. For private sessions, we can build exercises around your brand guidelines and current campaigns.

Which tools do you cover?

We cover general AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, plus AI features inside common marketing tools.

Is there support after the course?

Yes. We offer optional follow-up sessions to review progress and answer questions as your team puts AI into practice.

Do attendees need experience with AI tools?

Some experience helps, but it is not essential. We cover the basics on day one before moving on to marketing workflows.

Can this course be delivered on site?

Yes. Private sessions can be delivered at your premises over two days or as four half-day sessions.
