Cookie policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains how ICE Media Group Ltd uses cookies and similar technologies on this website, in line with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) and UK GDPR.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in the same way. They help a website work and can show the owner how it is used.

How we use cookies

We use essential cookies to run the website and remember your cookie choice. We use analytics cookies only if you select "Accept all". We do not use advertising cookies on this website.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address to deliver the font files. Google Fonts does not set cookies.

Changing your settings

You can change your choice at any time using the button below or the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect how the website works.
